Conditions

Last updated: 28 October 2026

These conditions apply whenever you use Aurora to download YouTube videos, Shorts, playlists or channels as MP3 audio or MP4 video. Read them together with the Terms of Use.

1. Local storage only

Aurora writes files directly to a folder you select on your own machine. It has no cloud storage, no account, and no analytics. Your download history stays in the application's own memory on your device.

2. Folder access

The folder browser can only be used from your own computer. Requests coming from any other machine are refused, so the drive listing is never exposed to the network. If you bind the server to your local network, downloading still works, but folder browsing, folder creation and "Show in folder" are disabled for remote visitors.

3. Copyright and fair use

You are responsible for every file you download. Condition of use: you will only save content that you own, that is offered for download, that is in the public domain, or that you are otherwise legally entitled to copy in your jurisdiction.

4. Format conversion

MP3 extraction and MP4 stream merging are performed locally with ffmpeg. Conversion does not improve the source quality — an MP3 is limited to the audio YouTube serves, and an MP4 can never exceed the video's original resolution.

5. Availability

Downloads depend on third-party infrastructure we do not control. Videos may be private, deleted, region-locked, age-restricted or unavailable at any moment; Aurora cannot guarantee that a link will work.

6. No tracking

Aurora sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. Thumbnails come straight from YouTube's image servers; nothing else leaves your machine except the requests needed to fetch the videos you asked for.

7. Changes

We may modify these conditions as the application changes. Continued use after a change means you accept the new conditions.
